# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 26 September 2026

EV Location Finder is designed to help people discover electric-vehicle
charging locations and plan routes. This policy explains the information the
service may process.

## Information we process

We may process search and route inputs, approximate location data supplied by
the user, device and diagnostic data needed to operate the service, and
account or support details when those features are enabled. We do not ask for
precise location access unless the user chooses to provide it.

## Use and sharing

We use information to provide search and routing, protect the service, fix
errors, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal information.
Information may be shared with infrastructure, mapping, payment, or hosting
providers only when needed to provide an enabled feature and under appropriate
confidentiality obligations.

## Retention and choices

We retain information only as long as needed for the stated purpose, legal
requirements, or security records. Users may request access, correction, or
deletion of personal information by contacting the service operator. Browser
and device controls can also be used to disable optional location access.

## Security and changes

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online
service can guarantee absolute security. We will update this policy when our
processing changes and will publish the revised effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue at
https://github.com/tharankanth03/ev-location-finder/issues.
